SYSTEM MANUAL

How battles work.

BattleLab uses real base statistics from PokéAPI, then turns them into a short best-of-three challenge.

01

Choose a squad

Use the three dropdowns on the play screen. A Pokémon can only occupy one slot, and the type filter narrows every list.

02

Meet the rival

The game randomly chooses three different opponents from the roster after your team is locked.

03

Compare stats

Every matchup randomly selects three of six stats: HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, and Speed.

04

Win the match

The fighter winning most stat comparisons takes the round. The team winning most of the three rounds takes the battle.
